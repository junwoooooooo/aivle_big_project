## Contracts implemented

- Added canonical internal task types `CONCEPT_FACTORY_RUN`, `CONCEPT_CANDIDATE`, `CONCEPT_LEGAL_REVIEW`, and `CONCEPT_REDESIGN`.
- Candidate and redesign Provider DTOs are closed schemas containing the required concept fields and exclude evidence IDs, legal text, final legal status, user-confirmed state, and trace fields.
- Legal review receives one shared Legal Context per Idea Brief snapshot. It includes industry, region, platform role, transaction/payment/data/physical activity context, qualifications/permits, labeling/advertising, and server-owned official Evidence references.
- Legal Provider output uses numeric indexes into the supplied official Evidence list. Unknown or duplicate references are rejected; empty Evidence cannot pass review.
- Origin Trace and Legal Trace are generated by the backend and persisted separately from Provider output.
- V9 persists the eight D-009 Attempt error classifications, safe error code, retryability, result JSON, shared context fields, trace JSON, and orchestration TaskRun linkage.
- Run creation now atomically queues a durable `CONCEPT_FACTORY_RUN` TaskRun and emits `job.concept.run.queued`.
- The Worker emits safe run/slot events, performs one schema repair, one transient retry, one legal redesign, and at most two replacement rounds.
- `IMPLEMENTABLE` and `IMPLEMENTABLE_WITH_CONTROLS` become eligible. `NEEDS_FACTS` pauses the run. `REDESIGNABLE` gets one redesign. `REJECTED` is safely summarized and replaced.
- Slot processing is isolated: a failed Slot does not roll back or discard already eligible or independently successful Slots. Any unresolved Slot failure terminates the parent TaskRun instead of leaving it RUNNING.
